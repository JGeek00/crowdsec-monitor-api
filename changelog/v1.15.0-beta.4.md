# v1.15.0-beta.4

## Added

- Alert details in your notifications: when creating or editing a notification, you can now turn on "Include alert information". Every notification will then carry the details of the alerts that triggered it, right in the message body.
- You stay in control of where those details go: only delivery channels that support rich alert details will include them, while the rest keep receiving your plain message. The app shows you which of your channels support it, and the option explains this as you set it up.

## Notes

- Alert details are included when the notification is delivered through an email channel. Other channels will support them in future releases.
- This is a beta release: notification behavior may still be refined before the final release.
