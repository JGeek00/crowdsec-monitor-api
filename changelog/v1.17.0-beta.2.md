# v1.17.0-beta.2

## New

- Every alert now tells you what kind it is: the alert detail (and list) includes a `kind` field — `waf`, `bot-detection`, `crowdsec`, and so on — so apps can show at a glance whether something was a web attack, a bot challenge or a classic log-based detection.
- Web-attack alerts keep showing their rule, targeted site, request method and URI exactly as before; the kind is simply added on top.

## Notes

- This is a beta: the new field may still be refined before the stable release. Your stored alerts and settings are untouched.
- The `kind` of alerts synced before this version appears after the next automatic sync fills it in; until then it reads as empty, and everything else keeps working exactly as before.
- No configuration changes are needed.
